Case study: 98 CSV files into one reliable table

The problem

A maritime research team kept vessel tracking history in 98 separate CSV files with duplicates, inconsistent schemas and timestamps that had no time zone, so events could not be ordered reliably across regions.

What I did

  • Built a SQL extraction pipeline that cleaned and merged all 98 files (48,510 tracking events).
  • Applied 8 targeted fixes to a 15,900+ row encounter dataset, including deduplication, unique indexing and schema standardization.
  • Converted timestamps to time zone aware formats and added integrity constraints to the tracking tables.
  • Validated the merged output against the source files before handing it over.

The result

One consistent, constraint-protected dataset where duplicates cannot re-enter and event times stay accurate across regions.

Case study: investigating ownership and identity gaps

I cross-reference global registries such as Equasis, GISIS and the Tokyo MOU, plus corporate registries in the Philippines, Hong Kong and China, to find hull registration details, ownership links and tracking gaps. Each finding in a report lists its source so a client can verify it.
